Our Story

A Munich Original,
Since Day One.

Steinheil 16 isn't just a restaurant — it's a Munich institution. Tucked in the heart of Maxvorstadt, a stone's throw from the Pinakotheken art museums and just 100 metres from the TU München, we've been serving up generous plates and cold Augustiner beer for over two decades.

What started as a neighbourhood Wirtshaus has become one of the city's most-mentioned spots in travel guides, food blogs, and late-night conversations. Locals, students, professors, and visitors from every corner of the world walk through our doors — and most of them leave talking about the Schnitzel.

Our kitchen runs daily specials alongside the regular menu, so there's always something different to try. We also host rotating art exhibitions on our walls, curated with the Kunstverein Gauting, making the space feel a little more alive every time you visit.

Getting Here

We're right in Maxvorstadt — close to the Pinakotheken, Königsplatz, and TU München. Public transport is easiest: U2 to Theresienstraße, then a short walk. Street parking is available nearby.
